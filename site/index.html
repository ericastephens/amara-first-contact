Solution

Triage and referral at the drug shop. Offline.

The small AI

Small enough for a basic phone. Honest when unsure.

A 660 KB model reads her Swahili words with no internet. It picks from a fixed list of symptoms and exposures. Below 40% confidence it says “Not sure”. It never decides urgency.

Who it helps

Mothers

The right clinic, first time

Clinic, time and code by SMS. No diagnosis, no health details.

Clinicians

Up-to-date guidance

Her note arrives first, with conditions to rule out and draft ICD-10 codes. Rules follow Tanzanian and WHO guidelines, cite their source, and update on every phone at the next sync.

District & regional health officers

Outbreaks seen early

Anonymous weekly counts by ward. Rising clusters alert nearby clinics and the surveillance officer, who approves each escalation.
